# Code of Conduct

SnowShoe is a place where builders of different backgrounds and experience levels work together. This code applies in the repository, in issues, in pull requests, in community channels for the project, and at project events.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Explain your reasoning. Prefer concrete suggestions over vague objections.
- Welcome newcomers. Answer questions without condescension.
- Credit other people's work.
- Write in clear language. Use translation tools if you need them, and ask for help if you are unsure.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing private information.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or token shilling.
- Submitting content you know is false or unsafe, or that you do not have the right to share.
- Pressuring maintainers or reviewers for faster approval.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, and restrict or remove access when behavior violates this code.

Report a concern to any Core Maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), or through the private reporting form on the Security tab. Reports are handled confidentially. The people involved in a report do not take part in its review.

Typical responses, in increasing order: a private reminder, a public correction, a temporary suspension, a permanent removal.
